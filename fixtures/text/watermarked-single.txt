there is "no way to maintain information about the collection because, in all cases, it was previously maintained in secrecy".

It also says that "an attempt to conceal the history of data collection under [The Government's Public Records Act] will not achieve the goal of safeguarding public trust and secrecy".

The library says that it will now publish all its published information to a public library, but that "this is not to preclude public scrutiny to make sure that our new policy is in keeping with the policy of the government".

The government wants the archive to become "consistent, efficient, complete and independent" and to "provide information the Library could access, based on their own assessment of their information and the existing requirements provided for archive stewardship and transparency".

"Archives can no longer hide information from government agencies or other stakeholders," it says.

The government did not respond to request for comment.
